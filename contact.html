Client Privacy Policy

Haven & Crest operates under strict confidentiality protocols. We never sell, monetize, or publicly disclose client identifiers, financial holdings, or acquisition inquiries.

  • Blind trust and entity representation protocol
  • Strict non-disclosure agreement (NDA) enforcement

Our agency advisory agreements conform to the highest California and National Association of Realtors fiduciary standards, ensuring absolute loyalty and disclosure.
